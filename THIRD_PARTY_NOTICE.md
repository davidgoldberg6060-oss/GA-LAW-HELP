# Third-Party Rights and Attribution Register

STANDALONE ASSISTANT GA.LAW.HELP includes or may interact with third-party software, services, legal-source material, and platform infrastructure. Their respective licenses and terms remain controlling.

## Current categories requiring license/terms tracking

- Android / AndroidX components
- Kotlin and Gradle build tooling
- Google Material components
- Python and backend dependencies listed in backend/requirements.txt
- JavaScript / frontend dependencies listed in package manifests and lockfiles
- Hosting, database, payment, AI/model, email, authentication, and app-store services configured for deployment
- Georgia statutes, court opinions, government forms, and other legal-source materials

## Release rule

Before a public commercial release, maintain a dependency/license inventory and satisfy any attribution, notice, source-disclosure, trademark, API, data-use, or redistribution obligations applicable to each third-party component.

Nothing in the project's proprietary LICENSE is intended to relicense third-party materials or public-domain government works.
