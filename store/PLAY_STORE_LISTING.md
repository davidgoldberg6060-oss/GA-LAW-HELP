## Full description
GA Law Help puts Georgia's legal code, published court opinions, and state regulations in your pocket. Search by keyword, browse by topic, or open a Legal Loopholes section that flags common legal misconceptions people search for.

What's inside:
- 232,803 Georgia laws, court opinions, and regulations
- Fast full-text search with relevant results
- Topic browsing for statutes, regulations, and opinions
- A Legal Loopholes section covering frequent misconceptions
- No account required to search; accounts unlock saved history

This is a legal reference tool. It is not legal advice and not a substitute for an attorney. For anything involving your rights, obligations, or a dispute, talk to a licensed Georgia lawyer.
