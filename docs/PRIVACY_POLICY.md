# GA.LAW.HELP Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

GA.LAW.HELP is an app that gives general information about Georgia law. It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. This policy explains what the Android app, the website and the GA.LAW.HELP server collect, why, and how you can delete it.

## What we collect

### If you use the app without an account
- **Questions you type or dictate.** Guest questions (for example Know Your Rights or Georgia law searches) are sent to the GA.LAW.HELP server to search our library of Georgia law and, for some features, to an AI provider to write an answer. Guest questions are not linked to an account.

### If you create an account
- **Account details:** your email address, a one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself), your account type, when you verified your email, and when the account was created.
- **Sign-in sessions:** hashed refresh tokens and one-time email verification and password reset tokens, each with an expiry time.
- **Case information you enter:** case titles, case numbers, county, case type, stage, the description of what happened, timeline events, saved legal research notes, and the Case Chat, Legal Loopholes and analysis results generated from them.
- **Files you upload:** documents, photos, audio or video you choose to add to a case, with the file name, file type, size and a SHA-256 integrity hash. Files are stored in private storage that is only reachable through the signed-in API.
- **Activity records:** a security log of account actions (for example sign-in, case created, file uploaded) with your user ID and the time. It does not contain the content of your cases.

### Stored only on your phone
- Your sign-in session, kept in Android encrypted storage.
- Your language choice, recent Georgia law searches, and the name and phone number you type on the Profile screen. These are not sent to our server.
- Talk to Me conversations are kept only while the app is open unless you choose to save them to a case.

### Microphone
The app asks for microphone access only when you tap the microphone button. Speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition, and only the resulting text is used. The app does not record in the background.

## How we use it
- To answer your questions and analyze your cases using verified Georgia legal sources.
- To sign you in, keep your account secure, verify your email and reset your password.
- To email you account messages (verification, password reset, and a confirmation receipt when you upload an audio or video file).

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.

## Who we share it with
We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app:
- **AI provider (OpenAI or xAI, depending on configuration):** the text of your question, and for case features the relevant case details you entered, to generate an answer.
- **Hosting and database (Railway) and file storage:** to store your account, cases and files.
- **Email delivery:** your email address and the message, to send account emails.
- **Payments (Stripe), only if paid plans are offered:** checkout is handled on Stripe's site; GA.LAW.HELP never sees or stores your card number. Paid plans are not offered during the free launch.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

## Security
Connections to the server use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed, sign-in tokens are hashed on the server and encrypted on your phone, and uploaded files are private to your account.

## Keeping and deleting your data
Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
- You can delete individual saved research notes inside a case.
- **Delete your account:** in the app, open Settings › Delete Account. You must enter your password and type DELETE. This permanently removes your account, all of your cases, notes, timeline events, analyses, uploaded files and sign-in tokens from our server. It cannot be undone. One anonymous record that an account was deleted is kept for security, with no user ID or email.
- **Clear App Data** in Settings removes the information stored on your phone but does not delete your account.

Copies held by service providers (for example AI provider request logs, or standard server logs kept by our host, which may include IP addresses) are kept under those providers' own retention policies.

## Children
GA.LAW.HELP is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and show the new version in the app.
